Co-Solve Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Co-Solve is a word puzzle solving application for iOS. This privacy policy explains how information is handled when you use Co-Solve.

Information entered into Co-Solve

Words, guesses, letter constraints, puzzle results and other information you enter into Co-Solve are used to provide the application's word-solving functionality. Co-Solve does not require you to create an account.

Advertising

Co-Solve uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, interactions with advertisements and other diagnostic or usage information for purposes including providing, measuring and improving advertising.

Where required by applicable law, Co-Solve uses Google's consent management platform to request or manage your advertising privacy choices. The options available may depend on your country or region.

Third-party services

Co-Solve uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Information processed by these services is subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

For more information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services, visit: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

Word list downloads

Co-Solve may access the internet to download word lists and related word-frequency information used by its solving and recommendation features. These requests may necessarily transmit standard network information, such as an IP address, to the servers providing those files.

Data retention

Co-Solve does not operate a user account system or a developer-operated server for storing your puzzle guesses or puzzle history. Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to the policies of those services.

Children

Co-Solve is a general-audience word puzzle utility and is not specifically directed at children. Advertising and privacy choices may be affected by applicable laws, platform requirements and advertising service policies.

Your privacy choices

Where required, Co-Solve provides access to advertising privacy choices through Google's consent management system. You may also control certain privacy and advertising settings through iOS.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when Co-Solve's features, third-party services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy or Co-Solve can be submitted through the Co-Solve project support page: Co-Solve Support .
