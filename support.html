Co-Solve Support

Co-Solve is a companion for five- and six-letter word puzzles. It uses the feedback from your guesses to narrow the remaining possibilities and recommend useful next guesses.

Getting Started

Choose either five-letter or six-letter mode, enter your guess, then mark each letter according to the feedback from your puzzle. Co-Solve will automatically update the known pattern, required letters and excluded letters.

Word Lists

Co-Solve can download word lists and word-frequency information used to generate and rank candidate answers. An internet connection may be required when downloading or updating this data.

Having a Problem?

If Co-Solve is not behaving as expected, try resetting the current puzzle and entering your guesses again. Check that each letter has been assigned the correct feedback.

Privacy
